The guaranteed acquisition rules for Furniture Crafting are as follows.

  • Guaranteed first craft: If you craft while you own no furniture, decor, wallpaper, or floor items, the first item you obtain is always "Cozy Log Bed". With Craft ×10, the other 9 items follow the rates above.
  • The rates above apply in the same way to every craft, so you can obtain the same item more than once.
