The guaranteed acquisition rules for Teenieping Summon - Single Summon are as follows.

  • Guaranteed Rare character: If you have not obtained a Rare character in the previous 9 summons, the 10th summon always grants a Rare character.
  • Guaranteed Royal character: If you have not obtained a Royal character in the previous 124 summons, the 125th summon always grants a Royal character.
  • Guaranteed Legend character: If you have not obtained a Legend character while obtaining 14 Royal characters, you receive a Legend character instead of a Royal character at the moment you would obtain your 15th Royal character.
  • When you obtain a character of a given rarity, the guaranteed acquisition count for that rarity is reset.
